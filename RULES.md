# 🏆 Kabaddi: Raid Master — The Rulebook

Welcome to the mud court. One breath. One chant. Seven defenders standing between you and glory. Here's everything you need to know to raid like a legend. 🔥

---

## 🏟️ The Basics

- Two teams, **7 warriors each**, facing off across the mid-line.
- Teams take turns **raiding** — one brave soul crosses into enemy territory to steal points, while the other team tries to shut them down.
- A full match = **2 halves × 6 raids per team** = 24 raids of pure tension.
- Highest score when the dust settles wins the match. 🏆

## 🫁 The Chant — Your Lifeline

This is what separates Kabaddi from every other sport: **you can't inhale.**

- The moment you cross the line, you start chanting **"Kabaddi! Kabaddi!"** — and you cannot stop until you're safely home.
- Tap the **🗣️ Kabaddi!** button, or actually shout it into your mic (any loud chanting works — no need to nail the pronunciation).
- Let your breath run out mid-raid, and it's game over for that raid — **automatic OUT**, no matter how smooth your move was. 💀
- Bigger risks (Toe Touch, Bonus Raid) burn through your breath faster. Choose wisely.

## 🎯 The Moves

| Move | The Play | The Risk |
|---|---|---|
| ✋ **Hand Touch** | Quick tag, quick retreat. | Low risk, +1 point. Your bread and butter. |
| 👣 **Toe Touch** | Reach deep, tag two defenders in one breath. | High reward (+2), high danger. |
| 💨 **Dodge & Dash** | Ghost past the entire defense untouched. | Safe escape, but zero points if nobody's caught. |
| ⭐ **Bonus Raid** | Sprint for the bonus line and back. | Only unlocked when the defense is at full strength (6–7 players). +1 bonus point. |

## 🧮 How Points Happen

- 🖐️ **Touch point** — +1 per defender you tag.
- ⭐ **Bonus point** — +1 for crossing the bonus line and making it home.
- 🛑 **Tackle point** — the defense earns +1 whenever a raider gets caught, boxed in, or runs out of breath — and the **raider is OUT** (goes to the bench).
- 💥 **Super Tackle** — if the defense is down to **3 players or fewer** and still manages a tackle, they earn a heroic **+2** instead of +1.
- 🎉 **ALL OUT (Lona)** — wipe out an entire 7-player team in one go, and their rivals score a massive **+2 bonus** — plus the whole squad gets revived on the spot.

## 🔁 Revival — Nobody Stays Down Forever

- Every **touch/tackle point** scored revives that many benched players for the scoring team (Toe Touch +2 = 2 revived, Super Tackle +2 = 2 revived) — first one out, first one back in. **Bonus points do not revive anyone.**
- Momentum swings fast. A team that looked finished can claw its way back with one good raid.

## ⚠️ Do-or-Die — No More Playing It Safe

- Two raids in a row with **zero points**? The next one is a **Do-or-Die raid**.
- On a Do-or-Die raid, you **must score** — even a clean, safe escape (Dodge & Dash) counts as failure and you're ruled OUT.
- The screen glows red when this is happening. Feel the pressure. 🔴

## 🥇 Golden Raid — Sudden Death

- Tied after both halves? No draws here. It's **Golden Raid** time.
- One raid each, back and forth — **first team to score anything wins the match, instantly.** ⚡
- Miss? The other side gets their shot. Repeat until someone's raid seals the deal.

## 🏁 Full Time

- Higher score after 2 halves = the winner.
- Still tied? Golden Raid decides it. There's always a winner. 🏆
